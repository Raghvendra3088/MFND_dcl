1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORE RESEARCH TAKEAWAYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Faithful Base Paper Reproduction:
   Our reproduction of the base paper (Qian et al., 2026) achieved 95.97% +/- 0.37% 
   accuracy, successfully matching and exceeding the paper's reported 95.50%.

2. Discovery of the Temporal Shortcut (Audit Finding):
   A critical forensic audit of the released base paper codebase revealed that 
   the >95% accuracy was driven by an undocumented 2-dimensional timestamp feature 
   ("hour=14"). When tested in isolation, this temporal shortcut alone produced 
   90.48% accuracy without any image or text features! When metadata is removed, 
   the true multimodal baseline of the base paper drops by 6.72% to 89.25%.

3. Genuine Multimodal Improvement (Our CFCA-I Novelty):
   Under the strict, clean protocol (zero metadata shortcut, text and image only), 
   our proposed Claim-aware Fine-grained Cross-modal Alignment (CFCA-I, M5a) 
   achieved 92.07% +/- 0.54% accuracy. This is a massive +2.82% absolute gain over 
   the clean baseline (McNemar Chi2 = 57.55, p = 3.30e-14, statistically proven).

4. Peak Architectural Configuration:
   Our fine-grained dual-max alignment architecture (CFCA-I + L_FG, M4) reached 
   93.33% +/- 0.63% accuracy and 93.32% Macro-F1, representing the highest overall 
   multimodal feature discrimination across all models tested.

5. External Benchmark Reality Check (RoViT-Detect Reproduction):
   The recent RoViT-Detect paper (Bhukya et al., 2026) claimed 98.93% on Weibo 
   using RoBERTa + ViT late fusion. Under our audited, deduplicated clean test set, 
   its true reproduction accuracy is 85.17% +/- 0.53%. Our hard-negative extension 
   (HN-RoViT) improved this to 85.54% +/- 0.29%, cutting variance nearly in half.

Detailed Step-by-Step Ablation Insights:

1. Cross-Attention vs Global Pooling (Baseline 89.25% -> M2 91.79%):
   Replacing global pooling with gated cross-attention produces a +2.54% boost. 
   Allowing words to directly attend to visual patches reveals localized evidence.

2. Impact of Claim and Region Importance Heads (M2 91.79% -> M3 91.99%):
   Adding learnable importance weights (a_i for words, b_j for patches) provides 
   a +0.20% gain. It suppresses uninformative padding tokens and highlights 
   high-entropy rumor keywords.

3. Massive Impact of Fine-Grained Alignment Loss L_FG (M3 91.99% -> M4 93.33%):
   Adding token-patch dual-max InfoNCE loss produces an immediate +1.34% jump 
   (McNemar Chi2 = 25.967, p = 3.47e-07). This is the single strongest learning 
   signal in the framework, forcing every word to find its exact visual region.

4. Role of Inconsistency Head (M4 93.33% vs M5 92.71%):
   Adding the auxiliary self-supervised mismatch classifier (L_inc) slightly 
   shifts the decision threshold (-0.62%, p = 0.015). For presentation and paper, 
   M4 is the recommended best model, with M5 documented in the ablation ladder.

5. Mathematical Necessity of Dual-Max vs Bilinear Sum Collapse (M6 Control):
   M6 replaces max-pooling with sum-pooling. While the mean appears high (92.86%), 
   its standard deviation explodes to +/- 1.42% (individual seeds: 90.58% to 94.34%). 
   Mathematically, sum-pooling collapses cross-attention into an unweighted dot 
   product between global sums. Dual-max alignment stabilizes representation 
   learning, cutting variance by 2.3x (+/- 0.63%) and guaranteeing convergence.

6. WHAT IS IMPROVED FROM BASE PAPER IMPLEMENTATION & NOVELTY
================================================================================

A. Shortcomings of Base Paper (MFND-DCL):
1. Single Vector Bottleneck: The base paper compressed an entire text post and 
   image into single global 1024-d vectors, causing severe information loss.
2. Inability to Detect Local Misinformation: Rumors often contain an authentic, 
   unaltered image paired with an exaggerated fake claim. Global pooling fails 
   to identify whether specific words match specific image regions.
3. Hidden Reliance on Timestamp Shortcut: Base paper performance dropped by 
   6.72% when the undocumented hour feature was removed.

B. Our Architectural & Methodological Novelty (CFCA-I):
1. Token-to-Patch Cross-Modal Interaction:
   Instead of global pooling, we extract L=64 text tokens and 7x7=49 image 
   patches from Chinese-CLIP ViT-H/14. Words query image patches, and image 
   patches query text claims.
2. Dual-Bottleneck Projection:
   Channels are compressed from 1024 -> 512 -> 128 dimensions, preventing 
   overfitting and accelerating matrix multiplication.
3. Claim-aware and Region Importance Heads:
   Learnable attention weights a_i and b_j identify high-entropy entities and 
   suppress irrelevant background tokens.
4. Symmetric Dual-Max Fine-Grained Alignment Loss (L_FG):
   For every word token, the loss locates the single most semantically matching 
   image patch (max_j) rather than averaging across the whole image.
5. Cross-Modal Inconsistency Head (L_inc):
   A self-supervised mismatch discriminator that detects manipulated text-image pairs.
