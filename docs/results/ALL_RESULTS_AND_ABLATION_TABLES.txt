Component-by-Component Analysis for Table 2:
1. Gated Cross-Attention (M0a -> M2):
   Replacing global pooling with token-patch cross-attention yields the largest single jump (+2.54%), proving that 
   local word-to-region interaction is foundational.
2. Claim & Region Importance Heads (M2 -> M3):
   Adding learnable importance weights a_i and b_j contributes +0.20% by suppressing irrelevant padding and background patches.
3. Dual-Max Alignment Loss L_FG (M3 -> M4):
   Symmetric word-to-region InfoNCE alignment provides a massive +1.34% boost (91.99% -> 93.33%, p = 3.47e-07).
4. Multi-Scale Context Pooling (E0 -> E1):
   Concatenating global scene CLS embeddings with attended local patch vectors adds +1.68% (90.53% -> 92.21%).
5. Deep Dual-Backbone Ensembling (E1 -> E2):
   Fusing Chinese-RoBERTa syntax parsing stabilizes representations, cutting seed variance from +/- 1.06% down to +/- 0.41%.
6. Mathematical Collapse Control (M6 vs M4):
   M6 replaces Dual-Max alignment with bilinear sum pooling. The accuracy has high variance (+/- 1.42%), proving mathematically 
   that bilinear sum pooling collapses into an unweighted dot product, whereas Dual-Max guarantees stable convergence.
